## Slide 4: App Creation for Developers

# Meta Developer Platform

### The challenge
Confusing platform with technical jargon led developers to request unnecessary permissions, creating privacy risks. High can_access but low did_access rates.

### The solution
- Organized 100+ permissions into 19 developer-focused use cases
- Created scalable platform style guide
- Integrated legal/compliance disclosures into workflow
- Applied plain language principles and progressive disclosure

### Impact
- **42% increase** in did_access rates
- **Reduced app ads creation** from 46 steps on 4 platforms to 20 steps on 2 platforms

## Slide 8: Key Methodology

# My Approach to Content Design

### User Research → Data Analysis → Cross-Functional Collaboration

**Discovery**
- Interview customers and analyze support data to find pain points
- Audit existing content, terminology, and navigation patterns
- Identify high-risk discrepancies and areas for improvement

**Collaboration**
- Partner with design, research, legal, policy, and privacy teams
- Align with product teams to eliminate siloed language
- Work cross-functionally to balance compliance with user experience

**Execution**
- Apply plain language principles to complex terminology
- Build scalable systems with reusable patterns
- Create taxonomies grouped by user goals, not technical implementation
- Design progressive disclosure strategies
- Craft contextual microcopy to reduce cognitive load

**Iteration**
- Test and iterate based on metrics (can_access vs did_access, drop-off rates, support volume)
- Establish governance frameworks for long-term consistency
- Collect feedback through surveys and continuous improvement loops
