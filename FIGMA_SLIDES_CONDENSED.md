## Slide 7: Designing Compliance - Overview

# Designing Compliance
**Meta**

### App Review Submission
**Challenge:** Opaque tracking, missing confirmations  
**Solution:** Progress indicators, confirmation modals, automated notifications  
**Impact:** 90% reduction in time-to-submission (36 days → 4 days)

### Data Access Forms
**Challenge:** Jargon, inconsistent terminology  
**Solution:** Standardized text, conversational tone, contextual tools  
**Impact:** Smoother submissions, less back-and-forth

### App Violations
**Challenge:** Unclear remediation steps  
**Solution:** Multi-level status, direct links, tailored guidance  
**Impact:** Fewer appeals, less "Need More Info" requests

## Slide 11: Meta - Cross-Platform Taxonomy

# Cross-Platform Term Audit & IA Alignment

### The challenge
Meta's 5 developer centers (Social, Horizon, Wearables, Llama, AI) each had different terminology and navigation. Over 70 overlapping concepts described differently.

### The approach
- Comprehensive audit across all platforms
- Identified 17 high-risk discrepancies
- Standardized terms (consolidated "Home," "Dashboard," "My Apps" → unified "Home")
- Aligned navigation structures
