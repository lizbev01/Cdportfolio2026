## SLIDE 10: App Creation - The Challenge

### The challenge

Meta's developer platform app creation was confusing and filled with technical jargon without guidance. Developers didn't understand requirements, leading them to request broad data access they didn't use.

## SLIDE 13: App Creation - Permissions Highlight
**Layout:** Problem-solution format

**Content:**
### Highlight: permissions, features and use cases

**The challenge**
Over 100 unorganized permissions with technical names plus 45 products overwhelmed developers. They relied on incomplete documentation or external forums.

Multiple Meta platforms were required for simple integrations like app ads, causing over-permissioning, unclear developer intent, and complex federal reporting.

**The approach**
- Partnered with Data Science to analyze historical access data (can_access & did_access rates), identifying commonly co-used permissions, features, and products
- Aligned with 19 internal product teams on integration requirements
- Created use cases from requirements, reducing choices and cognitive overload (applying Hick's Law)
- Authored a platform style guide for consistent future use case content and UI
- Connected existing platforms and terminology guides for common developer tasks

## SLIDE 19: Compliance Case Study 1 - App Review Submission
**Layout:** Challenge-approach-impact

**Content:**
### Case study 1: App Review submission

**The challenge**
Developers experienced friction from opaque tracking, missing confirmations, and unclear legal terms, eroding trust and delaying revenue while increasing Meta's compliance risk.

**The approach**
- **Progress tracking:** Green checkmarks as clear completion indicators at every step
- **Final step clarity:** Comprehensive confirmation modal summarizing all key information before submission
- **Error prevention:** Locked forms post-submission; embedded direct links to legal terms
- **Notification system:** Automated email and push notifications for immediate confirmation
- **Continuous improvement loop:** Optional post-submission survey for ongoing feedback

## SLIDE 21: Compliance Case Study 2 - Data Access Forms
**Layout:** Challenge-approach-impact

**Content:**
### Case study 2: data access request forms

**The challenge**
App Review forms contained jargon, inconsistent terminology, and hidden requirements. Upload tools at bottom of long forms overwhelmed developers.

**The approach**
- **Standardization at scale:** Analyzed 100+ permissions to create reusable text strings ensuring consistency
- **Tonal recalibration:** Shifted from formal legal tone to conversational yet serious language
- **Structural reorganization:** Relocated upload tools to contextually relevant sections
- **Modal implementation:** Allowed developers to complete requirements without leaving the page

**The impact**
- Smoother developer experience with easier submission tracking
- Reduced back-and-forth through first-submission requirement completion

## SLIDE 22: Compliance Case Study 3 - App Violations
**Layout:** Challenge-approach-impact

**Content:**
### Case study 3: app violations in App Review

**The challenge**
Compliance experience confused developers whose apps violated rules through lack of context, inconsistent terms, and unclear remediation steps, cutting data access and partner revenue.

**The approach**
- **Multi-level status visibility:** Exact submission status at app-level and permission-level
- **Language softening:** "Not approved" instead of "Rejected"
- **Comprehensive violation context:** Specific reason, policy broken, data access cutoff date
- **Smart navigation:** Direct links to pre-filled problematic submission sections
- **Proactive remediation guidance:** Recommended changes tailored to each violation
- **Immediate feedback loop:** Automated receipt confirmation upon resubmission

**The impact**
- Clearer developer expectations for rejections
- Decreased communications needed for appeal resolution
- Downward trend in Need More Information requests

## SLIDE 26: Meta - Cross-Platform Term Audit
**Layout:** Challenge-approach

**Content:**
### Cross-platform term audit and IA alignment

**The challenge**
Meta's developer centers operated in silos, each creating its own terminology and navigation patterns. This fragmented experience confused developers working across multiple platforms and made Meta appear disorganized and difficult to work with.

**The approach**
- **Comprehensive cross-platform audit:** Systematically audited terminology and IA across all five Meta developer centers
- **Pattern identification at scale:** Discovered over 70 overlapping concepts being described with different terms
- **Risk prioritization:** Isolated top 17 high-risk discrepancies actively causing confusion
- **Cross-functional alignment:** Partnered with product teams to eliminate siloed language
- **Terminology standardization:** Established single sources of truth (consolidated "Home," "Dashboard," "My Apps" into unified "Home")
- **Feature disambiguation:** Clarified conflicting feature names like "Required Actions" vs "Tasks"
- **Structural consistency:** Aligned top and left navigation layouts, documentation hierarchies, and interface elements

**Colors:**
- Primary: Teal/dark teal (#0D7377 or similar for --teal-dark)
- Secondary: Teal accent (lighter shade for --teal-accent)
- Neutrals: White, neutral grays (50, 100, 200)
- Text: High contrast dark on light backgrounds

- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Generous tracking for headings and eyebrow text
- Body text with good line-height for readability

- Minimalist, professional aesthetic
- Generous white space

- Grid-based layouts
